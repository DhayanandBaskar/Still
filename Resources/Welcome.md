# The pleasure of a quiet page

There is a particular kind of clarity that comes from having less in front of you. A few good words, enough space around them, and the time to pay attention.

Still is a small place for that. Open a Markdown file, make yourself comfortable, and read.

## A slower kind of software

Your documents don’t need a dashboard. They need a thoughtful typeface, a comfortable line length, and a little breathing room.

> Simplicity is the space you leave for the things that matter.

This is a reading app. Keep writing in the editor you love; Still will follow along when you save.

## Make yourself at home

- Open a file with **⌘O**, or drop it into the window.
- Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** from the top-bar **Appearance** menu.
- Use **Aa** to choose your typeface and text size.
- Enter focus mode with **⇧⌘F**. Press **Escape** to return.
- Use the nested **Index** in the sidebar to find your place. **⇧⌘I** brings it into view.
- Collapse a branch, the whole Index, or Recent Files whenever you want more quiet.
- Use **⌘F** to find a word.
- Adjust the text with **⌘+** and **⌘−**.

### Room for the details

Code, tables, and lists get the same quiet treatment as prose.

```swift
let essentials = ["A good page", "A little space", "Time to read"]
for thing in essentials {
    print(thing)
}
```

| A small detail | A better reading experience |
| :--- | :--- |
| Comfortable measure | Lines that don’t stretch forever |
| Thoughtful spacing | Room for ideas to breathe |
| Remembered position | Pick up where you left off |
| Automatic refresh | Read what you just wrote |
